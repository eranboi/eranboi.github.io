In Immunica, YOU are the immune system. As pathogens invade your body, fight back with precise cursor control, automated weapons, and strategic upgrades.

## Core Gameplay

- **Active + Idle Hybrid** - No clicking required. Hover to deal damage while weapons fire automatically
- **Cursor-based combat** - Position yourself strategically as threats swarm in
- **Deep progression** - 186 upgrades across 6 categories

## Weapon Systems

- **Burst** - AOE pulse centered on your cursor
- **Missiles** - Homing projectiles that seek targets
- **Beam** - High-DPS laser for focused destruction
- **Drones** - Orbiting allies that intercept threats

## Features

- **Fever System** - Build kill streaks for up to +500% damage and 2x rewards
- **10 Pathogen Tiers** - From tiny Prions to massive Helminths, each with 3 evolution levels
- **30 Breach Modifiers** - Buffs, nerfs, and weird effects that change every run
- **12 Languages** - Including Japanese, Chinese, Turkish, and more
