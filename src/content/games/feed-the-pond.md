## The Loop

- **Feed the Wart** - Every bite pays you back, and the more it eats, the bigger (and weirder) it gets
- **Hire frogs** - Hatch tadpoles and put them to work catching flies, picking flowers and digging up mushrooms
- **Brew potions** - Two bubbling cauldrons turn simple ingredients into something the Wart loves even more
- **Find your combos** - Every ingredient does something different, and some are much stronger together
- **Start a new pond** - Keep what you've learned and grow faster every time, on the way to the Perfect Potion

## Features

- **Automation first** - Click to get started, then watch your frogs run the whole swamp
- **No single right answer** - Mix and match harvests and recipes to build a pond that's all yours
- **A real ending** - A short, sweet incremental with no endless grind
- **11 languages** - English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian and Turkish
