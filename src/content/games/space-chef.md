- **Fog of war** - Custom shader with persistent state, performance optimized
- **Restaurant customization** - Placement, validation and grid building systems
- **Gamepad support** - Full input mapping and platform optimizations
- **Minimap** - Dynamic minimap with real-time updates and fog of war integration
